Section 04

What's holding the site back

From a 100-page technical crawl (on-page score: 70.5/100) plus manual inspection of all 14 core pages. Grouped by severity.

Critical

No SEO plugin installed - the site has never been set up for search

The site serves WordPress's default sitemap, titles follow the default "Page - Site Title" pattern, and meta descriptions are absent or auto-scraped page text. There is no keyword targeting anywhere on the site.

Critical

Every page title ends in "- Fodder Link Website"

The WordPress site title is literally "Fodder Link Website". The homepage title is "Fodder Link Website - Quality feed, tested and delivered". Not one page title targets a search term buyers actually use.

Critical

H1 headings broken on every page

69 of 100 crawled pages have no H1 at all (every batch page, contact, team and quote pages). Core pages that do have an H1 render it twice - a theme template fault. Google is left guessing what each page is about.

Critical

Meta descriptions missing or broken on 70 of 100 pages

Those that exist are duplicated page-text dumps of 300+ characters. The Request a Quote page's description is literally "Your Content Goes Here" - that is what a prospect may see under your listing in Google.

Critical

Wrong structured data everywhere it matters

The homepage tells Google it is a news article (NewsArticle schema). Batch listings carry no Product or Offer schema, there is no Organization schema, and no LocalBusiness markup - so the site is invisible to rich results.

High

98 broken internal links; 253 externally-linked pages returning errors

A legacy /batch/[id]/ URL pattern was never redirected when the site moved to its current structure. 16 of the 17 dead URLs found in the crawl follow this one pattern - a single rewrite rule fixes it and recovers the link value now being thrown away.

High

353 of 373 sitemap URLs are near-identical thin batch pages

Each carries ~400 words of boilerplate; the crawler flags 40% duplicate content. There is no lifecycle handling for sold or expired batches. The fix is also the opportunity: each batch's feed-test values (protein, NDF) are unique data no competitor page has - surfacing them as indexable text makes every listing distinct.

High

The backlink profile is effectively a cold start

52 referring domains, but only three or four carry any relevant weight (feedlots.com.au, beefex.com.au). The rest are URL shorteners and link directories, with an average spam score of 51/100. Domain rank: 96 out of 1,000.

High

Content stopped 18 months ago - and one live post is a sample slug

Three blog posts total, newest December 2024. The December market update is published at the URL "this-is-a-sample-post". Meanwhile the one real article ranks page two for "feed test" on its own.

High

No image alt text on 82 of 100 pages

Batch photos and category images carry no alt attributes - lost relevance signals and an accessibility gap, fixable at template level.

Healthy

The technical foundation is solid - this is fixable, not a rebuild

Valid HTTPS with correct redirects, sane robots.txt, canonicals in place, no accidental noindex, Lighthouse mobile scores of 92 (SEO), 95 (accessibility) and 100 (best practices), and fast lab load times (LCP 165 ms). The plumbing works; the signals are what's missing.

Section 05

The 90-day priority plan

In order. The P1 items are one-off fixes that stop the leaks; P2 builds the visibility; P3 compounds it.

P1

Foundations

weeks 1-2
  1. Install an SEO plugin (Rank Math or Yoast). Change the site title to "FodderLink". Write keyword-targeted titles and meta descriptions for the 11 core pages - the homepage should target hay and straw for sale, feed tested, Australia-wide.
  2. Redirect the legacy /batch/[id]/ URLs with one rewrite rule - removes 98 broken internal links and recovers the value of 253 externally-linked dead pages.
  3. Fix the H1 structure: dedupe the double H1 in the theme, add H1s to batch, contact, team and quote templates.
  4. Fix the sample-post slug and redirect it.
  5. Set a batch lifecycle policy: expired batches redirect to their category; live batches get Product + Offer schema and their feed-test values as visible, indexable text.
P2

Visibility

months 1-2
  1. Build out category landing pages (hay, straw, other products) as the commercial targets for "cereal hay for sale", "straw for sale", "lucerne hay for sale".
  2. Restart the content program on the model that is winning this niche: monthly market updates (the inspector data already exists) plus evergreen guides - bale weights, hay vs silage, reading a feed test.
  3. Schema cleanup: Organization sitewide, Article on posts, remove NewsArticle from the homepage.
  4. Alt text on batch and category images at template level.
P3

Authority

ongoing
  1. Link building from the industry base: AFIA listing, ag associations, grower and buyer partners, rural press picking up the market updates. A handful of real agricultural links outweighs the entire current profile.
  2. Fix unnamed links and JavaScript-only filter anchors (crawlability and accessibility).
  3. Verify Google Business Profile status and set up Google Search Console before any performance reporting.
