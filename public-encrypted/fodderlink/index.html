The problem with buying feed blind

Good-looking hay can still let your herd down

Looks can deceive

Colour and smell don't tell you protein, energy or fibre. Hay that presents beautifully can under-deliver in the ration - and you only find out after your stock have been on it for weeks.

Private sales are a gamble

Classifieds and social media hay sales run on photos and trust. There are documented cases of buyers losing thousands in payment scams - and no test data, no declaration, no comeback when quality isn't as described.

The hidden chemical history

Without a Commodity Vendor Declaration you don't know what was sprayed, when, or whether withholding periods were met. That's a real risk to your livestock and your market access.

Common questions

Before you ask

Why don't you just list prices?

Because the right answer depends on freight. Hay is bulky - the delivered cost of a batch 100km away and one 800km away can differ substantially. We quote each inquiry with freight included so you're comparing real landed costs, not surprises at the weighbridge.

What does the feed test actually tell me?

The NIR analysis reports up to 30 nutritional values including protein, energy, fibre and moisture, so you (or your nutritionist) can slot a batch straight into a ration. Every listing publishes the results - and if you want help reading them, the team will explain what the numbers mean for your stock.

What is a Commodity Vendor Declaration and why should I care?

A CVD documents chemical treatments and withholding periods (WHP/ESI) for the batch. FodderLink won't list, market or purchase product without one. It's your protection against contaminated feed - and your evidence trail for meat and milk market access.

Do you deliver to my area?

Delivery runs Australia-wide through transport partners operating under approved mass and fatigue management schemes, with product protected in transit.

What if the batch isn't what I expected?

Every batch is sold on its published test results, inspection record and grade - the same information we buy on. If anything's off on arrival, the Contract & Logistics team addresses it directly with practical, transparent solutions.
