04 / 06What we are building

A site that wins tenders and backs the pitch.

Brett put it plainly: UBCQ needs a logo and a website so the team can push for tenders and show clients when pitching. Everything below is in service of that.

1 · Logo & brand guide

Finalise the UBCQ mark from Brett's mock-up and document it: colour, type, spacing, usage on site, documents and signage. Brett confirmed on 2 September that Growthify takes this on.

2 · Website design & build

WordPress site designed and developed by Growthify. Project pages for Serenity Reserve and Ivory Mt Gravatt, the 2031 vision and EVP as a careers story, and the TLPC lineage on the About page.

3 · Hosting

WP Engine, starting once initial designs are approved. Ongoing until cancelled.

4 · Maintenance & support

Technical and content maintenance, up to one hour per month, from launch.

To lock today: the invoicing entity (Unique Building Co QLD Pty Ltd or otherwise), and one approver at TLPC for design sign-off.
