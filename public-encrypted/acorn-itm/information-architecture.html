Website redesign · site structure
What we are proposing

From 37 pages
to 207.

The current site has no product structure at all - nothing for a builder searching “decking Hamilton” or “GIB Waikato” to land on. This is the page structure that fixes that: 18 categories, 151 sub-categories, plus the service, guide and compliance pages the site is missing.

No individual product pages. The site stays non-transactional - the full catalogue and trade pricing stay behind the existing trade login.

How it gets built

Three phases, ordered
by search demand.

The display order above follows how a job runs. The build order should follow keyword volume, which we size before starting.

01

Foundations

Homepage, 18 category pages, the service pages and the four compliance pages. This is the launch set and it alone takes the site from 37 to roughly 65 pages.

02

Depth

The 151 sub-categories, built highest-volume first. Panels & Wallboards and Frame & Truss lead: no local merchant currently ranks for GIB or plasterboard in Hamilton.

03

Content

Project guides and quantity calculators, then delivery-area pages once the footprint is confirmed. Highest-volume DIY search, lowest current coverage.
