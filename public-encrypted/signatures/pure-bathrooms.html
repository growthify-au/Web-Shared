Pure Bathrooms - Email Signatures

Click Copy signature below, then paste it into your email settings. On the team signature, replace First Last, Position and the email address with your own.

Install in Gmail:
  1. Click Copy signature on the card you want below.
  2. Gmail > Settings (gear) > See all settings > General > Signature > Create new.
  3. Paste (Cmd+V / Ctrl+V), then type over the name, position and email.
  4. Set it as the default for new emails and replies, then Save.
Outlook: Settings > Mail > Compose and reply > paste into the signature box.
