Billiard Shop - Store Email Signatures

Find your store below and click Copy signature. Paste it into your email settings, then replace First Last and Position with your own name and role.

Install in Gmail:
  1. Click Copy signature for your store below.
  2. Gmail > Settings (gear) > See all settings > General > Signature > Create new.
  3. Paste (Cmd+V / Ctrl+V), then type over the name and position.
  4. Set it as the default for new emails and replies, then Save.
Outlook: Settings > Mail > Compose and reply > paste into the signature box.
