Start a Built with series

Every film follows five story beats.

Rob uses the StoryBrand method from Donald Miller's book, which many marketing teams already know. Your customer or your business is the hero. Your brand is the guide that helped them win. That's why it builds trust without sounding like an ad.

The beatIn a Legacy filmIn a Built with film
1The hero and what they wanted

The founder, and the business they set out to build.

Your customer, and the growth they were chasing.

2The problem that made it hard

The tough seasons, debts and near misses that almost ended it.

What stood in their way: cash flow, capacity, a market that changed.

3The guide who backed them

The staff, mentors and loyal customers who kept it going.

Your brand: the bank, supplier or partner who helped.

4How they did it

The decisions and hard work that turned it around.

What changed once they had the right support behind them.

5Where they are now

The business today, and what it means for the next generation.

You approve the story before we film a thing.

No surprises for you or your boss. The five beats are mapped out on one page and signed off before filming starts.

1Take the story quiz

Five minutes on where you started, the hard years, the key people and the milestones. Your story is half-built before we talk.

2Sign off the outline

A one-page outline of the five beats, with who says what in each. Nothing is filmed until you approve it.

3We film, on weekdays

Interviews with the people who matter, plus the place and the work itself. Planned around your business, so nobody stops trading.

4One film, 12 clips

The full documentary for your website and YouTube, plus 12 short clips ready for socials, ads and sales decks.
